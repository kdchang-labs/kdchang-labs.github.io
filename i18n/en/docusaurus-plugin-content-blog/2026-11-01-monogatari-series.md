# The KD LABs Monogatari Series: 3D Management Strategy Games You Can Play in Your Browser

> Open a boba shop, build a startup, run for mayor, scout the next ace. Every challenge is its own management strategy game.

Besides apps and browser extensions, KD LABs now makes **management strategy games**. We took challenges that feel familiar yet a little out of reach and turned them into a series of "Monogatari" (stories): resources are always short, every decision has a cost, and every run is a fresh start.

<!--truncate-->

## What the series offers

- **Play in your browser**: Nothing to download or install. Open the link on your computer and start playing.
- **Isometric 3D scenes**: Your shop, office, campaign headquarters or ballpark grows as you play.
- **Management × roguelite**: Random events, shops and build combinations make every run different, and you unlock new content across runs.
- **Chinese and English**: Every game is available in Traditional Chinese and English.

## Tea Bar Story

Start with a single night market cart. Develop recipes, hire staff and catch each week's trends. Five shops, each with a challenge week to clear. Make it through, and your little stall becomes a boba empire with lines around the world.

Play: [www.teabarstory.com](https://www.teabarstory.com/en)

## Garage to Unicorn: From a Garage Startup to a Billion-Dollar Valuation

Start in your parents' garage. Build the product, hire the team and win over investors. Cash burns every day, and you need to grow the company into a US$1 billion unicorn before it runs out.

Play: [www.garagetounicorn.com](https://www.garagetounicorn.com/en)

## 90 Days to City Hall

The mayoral election is ninety days away. Starting from a campaign office in a small storefront, plan your schedule, build a campaign team and shape your campaign strategy. Get through every stage and every surprise, and win New Harbor City on election night. Your first win unlocks governing mode: a four-year term to turn campaign promises into results, and then a run for re-election.

Play: [www.90daystocityhall.com](https://www.90daystocityhall.com/en)

## Next Baseball Ace: Sixteen Weeks to the Draft

You're the new scout for the Harbor Whales, a fictional pro baseball team. Spend sixteen weeks traveling to ballparks across the island to watch games, run tryouts and make home visits. Find prospects through the fog of their ratings, survive three scouting meetings, and pick the next ace at the draft. Whether you read them right, you'll find out three years later.

Play: [www.nextbaseballace.com](https://www.nextbaseballace.com/en)
