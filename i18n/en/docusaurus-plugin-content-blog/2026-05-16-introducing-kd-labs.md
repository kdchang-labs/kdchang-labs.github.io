# Introducing KD LABs: An Independent Product Studio

> Building products that solve real problems at work and in life, for a more open and better world.

## What is KD LABs?

I'm KD. I've worked as a senior engineer, a founder, a technical project manager and an engineering manager, spending about ten years in software development. Along the way I had a few small wins and plenty of failed attempts, and the most important thing I learned is this: **good products come from understanding what users need, and from putting ideas in front of real users as early as possible.**

KD LABs is the independent product studio I founded. We focus on using technology to solve the problems people face at work and in life, turning our solutions into apps, browser extensions and web games, and we keep improving them based on people's feedback.

<!--truncate-->

## What we make

### Products: reading, learning and building your career

- **LifePicks**: Spin the gacha machine to pick a book by life theme. Every book comes with key takeaways and an action plan. Available on the web, iOS, Android and as a Chrome extension.
- **CareerTags**: Find your skill gaps based on your career goals, then turn what you learn from videos and podcasts into STAR stories you can tell in interviews, through practice and a practice journal.
- **CertGym**: Study key concepts organized by the official exam outline and work through past exam questions. Questions you get wrong go back to your review list automatically.

### Games: the Monogatari series of management strategy games

We turn running a shop, building a startup, winning an election and scouting for the draft into 3D management strategy games you can play in your browser. Every run brings different events and choices, and you have to make trade-offs with limited time and resources. Read more about the series in [this post](/blog/monogatari-series).

### Tools: small helpers for everyday work

- **HappyPrompt**: A Chrome extension that saves and inserts AI prompts in ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini in one click. Your data stays in your browser.

The full list is on the [Products](/products) page.
