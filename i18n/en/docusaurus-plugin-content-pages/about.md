# About

> Building products that solve real problems at work and in life, for a more open and better world.

## What is KD LABs

KD LABs is an independent product studio founded by [KD Chang](https://www.kdchang.com/). We focus on using technology to solve the problems people face at work and in life, and we keep improving our products based on their feedback.

KD has worked as a senior engineer, a founder, a technical project manager and an engineering manager, spending about ten years in software development and seeing projects both succeed and fail. That experience taught him that good products come from understanding what users need, and from putting ideas in front of real users as early as possible.

That's why KD LABs products are small, but each one starts from a real problem: wanting to read but not knowing which book to start with, learning workplace skills with no chance to practice them, or saving great AI prompts and never finding them again.

## What we make

### Products

- **[LifePicks](https://www.lifepicksbookstore.com/)**: An online indie bookstore that picks books by life theme. Spin the gacha machine to draw a book from six themes, each with key takeaways and an action plan, then add it to your shelf and track your reading. Available on the web, iOS, Android and as a Chrome extension.
- **[CareerTags](https://www.careertags.com/)**: Find your skill gaps based on your career goals, learn from curated videos and podcasts, then turn soft skills into visible results through practice, a practice journal and STAR stories. The Chrome extension is available now; iOS and Android apps are coming soon.
- **[ExploreStamp](https://www.explorestamp.com/en/)**: A stamp-collecting travel app. Explore sights through themed routes, check in by location at the spot to collect its stamp, and turn it into a postcard. No sign-in needed and works offline. iOS and Android versions are coming soon.

### Games

The Monogatari series of management strategy games. They run in your browser and are available in Chinese and English:

- **[Tea Bar Story](https://www.teabarstory.com/en)**: From a night market cart to a boba empire.
- **[Way to Unicorn](https://www.waytounicorn.com/en)**: From a garage startup to a billion-dollar valuation.
- **[90 Days to City Hall](https://www.90daystocityhall.com/en)**: A 90-day race to become mayor, followed by a four-year term if you win.
- **[Next Baseball Ace](https://www.nextbaseballace.com/en)**: Sixteen weeks of scouting ballparks across the island to pick the next ace at the draft.

### Tools

- **[HappyPrompt](https://www.happyprompt.net/)**: A Chrome extension for managing AI prompts. Save and insert prompts in one click, browse a prompt library for many roles, and keep all your data in your browser.

## How we build products

- **Solve one problem first**: Each product focuses on a single use case. We get that right before thinking about the next feature.
- **Respect your data**: If data can stay on your device, we don't upload it. If a feature can work without an account, we don't ask you to sign in.
- **Keep maintaining after launch**: After a product ships, we keep fixing and updating it based on feedback, and we share how we build things on the [blog](/blog).
